Our Services

Personally arranged childcare, wherever you travel.

Every arrangement is tailored to your family. There are no packages — only care prepared around your children, your plans and your traditions.

I.

Traveling Nannies

A dedicated caregiver accompanies your family from departure to return, providing continuity across flights, transfers, time zones and new surroundings.

  • Familiar, consistent care throughout your journey
  • Support with routines, rest and meals while in transit
  • Prepared in advance using your children's individual profiles
II.

Destination Nannies

Care that meets your family where you are staying — at your hotel, villa, resort or private residence — arranged around your plans for each day.

  • Care at your accommodation for the days and hours you need
  • Thoughtfully introduced so children feel at ease
  • Coordinated with your family's itinerary
III.

Jewish Family Childcare

AHUVAI specializes in serving Jewish families, including Orthodox families. Our approach emphasizes carefully chosen non-Jewish nannies who understand and respectfully accommodate your family's traditions and household practices.

  • Attentive to Shabbat and holiday routines
  • Respectful of kosher food requirements in your household
  • Mindful of modesty preferences, prayer times and Hebrew language use
  • Guided by how your family observes — never by assumption
IV.

Extended-Stay Care

For longer stays abroad, we arrange care that offers children continuity and a steady daily rhythm — so a season away can feel like home.

  • Consistent caregivers for longer periods
  • Daily routines built around school, rest and play
  • Communication with parents arranged around your preferences
