Privacy Notice

How we care for your family's information.

Families entrust us with personal details about their children. This notice explains what we collect, why, and how it is protected.

What we collect

Your account. When you create a family account, we collect your name, email address, optional phone number, and a securely hashed password.

Child profiles. In your private portal you may share a profile for each child, including personality, interests, routines, food and dietary information, allergies and other health information, comfort and sensory preferences, family and religious practices, and travel details. Parents decide what to share; many questions are optional.

Why we collect it

We use this information only to respond to you, to prepare and arrange childcare tailored to your children, and to communicate with you about that care. Health information is collected only with a parent or guardian's explicit consent, so that caregivers can keep your children safe.

Who can see it

Child profiles are visible only to the parent or guardian accounts for that family and to authorized AHUVAI administrators. Relevant details are shared with the caregivers arranged for your family only as needed to care for your children. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

How it is protected

  • All connections to the portal are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Questionnaire answers are additionally encrypted before they are stored, and the database is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider.
  • Access is restricted at the database level: a family account can only ever read its own records.
  • Administrator access requires a separate, protected account.
  • Email notifications never contain your children's profile details.
  • The portal uses only essential cookies needed to keep you signed in. It does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How long we keep it

We keep family information only for as long as needed to provide and follow up on care, after which it is securely deleted. You may ask us to delete your family's information at any time.

Your choices

You can review and update your children's profiles in the portal at any time. To request a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion, please contact us.
