Our Story

Care shaped around who your children are.

AHUVAI was founded on a simple belief: that every child deserves care shaped around their own personality, comforts and traditions — wherever in the world their family travels.

Our Philosophy

The quiet art of personal attention

Travel brings new places, new faces and new rhythms. For children, the difference between an unsettling journey and a joyful one is often a caregiver who truly knows them — who understands how they like to be comforted, what makes them laugh, and the routines that help them feel safe.

That is why AHUVAI begins every arrangement by listening. Before care is arranged, we take time to understand each child individually, and each family's way of life.

What Guides Us

Four commitments to every family

i.

Personal Attention

Each family receives genuinely individual care and a direct, personal relationship with AHUVAI — from the first conversation onward.

ii.

Cultural Understanding

We specialize in serving Jewish families, including Orthodox families, and welcome families of all backgrounds. We ask how your family observes — and never assume that every family observes in the same way.

iii.

Discretion

Families trust us with their travel plans and their children's details. We treat that trust with the discretion of a private family office.

iv.

Emotional Wellbeing

A child who feels understood can relax, explore and enjoy the journey. Emotional security is at the heart of everything we arrange.

The Name

In Hebrew, ahuvai means “my beloved ones.”

The care we arrange is guided by a single idea: that each child should be treated as the cherished individual they are.
