The AHUVAI Approach

Understanding each child is where good care begins.

Our approach is informed by what child development research has long shown: children thrive when the adults caring for them are warm, responsive and predictable — and when care fits the individual child.

Why We Ask

Getting to know your most cherished ones

Before care begins, parents complete a private profile for each child in our secure family portal. We ask about temperament, interests, daily routines, what comforts them, sensory preferences and family traditions.

This is not paperwork for its own sake. Each answer helps a caregiver respond to your child the way you would — from the first hello.

Evidence-Informed Care

What shapes every arrangement

i.

Temperament

Children differ from birth in how they approach new people, places and experiences. Some warm up slowly; others leap in. Knowing a child's temperament allows a caregiver to pace introductions and activities in a way that feels right for them.

ii.

Routines & Predictability

Consistent routines around sleep, meals and bedtime help children feel secure and regulate their emotions — especially through travel, time-zone changes and unfamiliar surroundings.

iii.

Responsive Relationships

Children feel safe with caregivers who notice their cues and respond warmly and consistently. This responsiveness is the foundation of trust between a child and a new caregiver.

iv.

Co-Regulation

When children are upset or overwhelmed, a calm adult helps them return to calm. Knowing what already soothes your child — a song, quiet time, a favourite toy — means comfort comes quickly.

v.

Interest-Led Play

Children engage most deeply when activities connect with what they already love. Their interests guide how a caregiver plans each day.

vi.

Sensory Awareness

Noise, crowds, textures and transitions affect children differently. Understanding sensory preferences helps caregivers anticipate and gently ease potential overwhelm.

Your Family, Your Way

Traditions are personal. So is our care.

Every family observes in its own way. Rather than assuming, we ask how your family lives — and prepare care that honours it.
